<p>题记 雨巷里的一把油纸伞</p> <br><p>一把带顶的雨伞，代码如下：</p> <br><pre><code class="language-cpp">%%%Date 2022年1月11日<br><br>%%%Author SmartGridequation<br><br>%%%Code BY MATLAB<br><br>figure(1)<br>draw_sphere();<br><br>function draw_sphere()<br><br>t=linspace(0,0.05*pi,2*pi);<br>p=linspace(0,2*pi,2*pi);<br>[theta,phi]=meshgrid(t,p); %网格化数据<br>R=1; %设置球面半径<br><br><br>x=R*sin(theta).*cos(phi); %代入参数方程<br>y=R*sin(theta).*sin(phi);<br>z=R*cos(theta);<br><br>hold on<br><br>deta = 0;<br>z = z*1.5;<br><br><br>for i = 1:20<br>deta = 0.001+deta;<br><br>surf(x/i,y/i,z+deta); %绘制表面图<br>end<br><br>end</code></pre> <br><p>效果如下：</p> <br><p>一把带顶的雨伞俯视图（一）</p> <br><p style="text-align:center;"><img alt="watermark,type_d3F5LXplbmhlaQ,shadow_50,text_Q1NETiBAU21hcnRHcmlkZXF1YXRpb24=,size_13,color_FFFFFF,t_70,g_se,x_16" src="https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/blog_migrate/cf9f5fd114e041fc69db1eef8f0310af.jpeg" /></p> <br><p> 一把带顶的雨伞侧视图（二）</p> <br><p style="text-align:center;"><img alt="watermark,type_d3F5LXplbmhlaQ,shadow_50,text_Q1NETiBAU21hcnRHcmlkZXF1YXRpb24=,size_13,color_FFFFFF,t_70,g_se,x_16" src="https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/blog_migrate/fcadf1168d5b783a8743d668e5a7697b.jpeg" /></p> <br><p></p> <br><p></p>
