<p>Python利用海龟画图turtle库做一个篮球比赛计时画面，代码如下</p> <br><pre><code class="language-python">import turtle<br>import time<br>import random<br>r = random.random()<br>g = random.random()<br>b = random.random()<br>turtle.speed(0)<br>for j in range(1,2,1):<br>    for i in range(1,60,1):<br>        print(i)<br>        time.sleep(0.1)<br>        turtle.color(r,g,b) #这是在设置颜色<br>        turtle.clear()<br>        turtle.hideturtle()<br>        turtle.penup()<br>        turtle.goto(0, 0)<br>        turtle.pendown()<br>        turtle.write('篮球比赛计时',align='center',font=('楷体',100,'normal'))<br>        turtle.penup()<br>        turtle.goto(60, -350)<br>        turtle.pendown()<br>        if(i<10):<br>            turtle.write('0'+str(j)+':0'+str(i),align='center',font=('楷体',300,'normal'))<br>        else:<br>            turtle.write('0'+str(j)+':'+str(i),align='center',font=('楷体',300,'normal'))<br>turtle.clear()            <br>turtle.write('计时结束',align='center',font=('楷体',100,'normal'))<br></code></pre> <br><p>效果如下</p> <br><p class="img-center"><img alt="" src="https://i-blog.csdnimg.cn/direct/413b35637c79481183cbdb9d533df0f7.gif" /></p>
